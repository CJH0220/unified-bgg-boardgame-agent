## 3. 核心机制亮点（1~3个关键）

### 1. Dice Rolling（掷骰）
- 机制定位：让随机结果参与决策，但不是把决策完全交给运气。
- 评价：骰子机制好不好玩，关键看它是在制造波动，还是在制造可管理的风险。
- 解析：评论里若反复出现“luck”“variance”“risk”，这套机制就是争议核心。

### 2. Variable Player Powers（可变角色能力）
- 机制定位：Variable Player Powers 会把本作的决策重心放在这条系统线上。
- 评价：它未必是最直观的卖点，但通常会影响整局游戏的节奏、风险和收益分布。
- 解析：评论若反复围绕这项机制讨论，说明它是本作体验的关键结构之一。

### 3. Cooperative Game（合作游戏）
- 机制定位：把玩家从对抗拉到共同解题，重点变成协作效率和沟通。
- 评价：合作机制的好坏主要看它能否提供持续的共同压力，而不是只靠主题。
- 解析：评论若强调“teamwork”“shared challenge”，通常说明协作结构很有存在感。

## 4. 游戏简介与说明

Forsaken Lore, an expansion for Eldritch Horror, thrusts investigators headlong into perilous and strange new encounters, while Ancient Ones defeated in the past attempt to reclaim the world. In order to stop them, investigators will need to uncover new mysteries, and dig even deeper into the lore surrounding each of the game's Ancient Ones with the help of new Research cards. Meanwhile, Yig's cultists and Serpent People work to prepare the way for the Father of Serpents to dominate our planet. With the help of powerful new Spells and Assets, along with mighty new Artifacts, Investigators must once again race across the globe in order to prevent humanity's destruction in Forsaken Lore.
